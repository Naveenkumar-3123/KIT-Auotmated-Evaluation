# OCR Accuracy Evaluator — Design Direction

## Ground-Truth Reference Interpretation

The uploaded interface is the reference for **atmosphere and interaction posture**, not for literal structure. The application will borrow its soft lavender perimeter, generous white central workspace, compact left rail, light material depth, deliberately offset cards, and friendly assistant presence. It will not replicate the prompt composer, conversational framing, wording, or chatbot behavior. The resulting product is a first-class OCR evaluation platform with a landing page that leads naturally into a technical comparison workspace.

## Style Decisions

The most recent uploaded page is now the **primary composition benchmark**. The landing screen will prioritize its viewport-filling lavender backdrop, inset frosted central panel, narrow circular tool rail, wide empty hero field, assistant-and-speech-bubble cluster, overlapping three-card rhythm, and compact bottom action dock. OCR content replaces all chat affordances: the action dock begins document intake, feature cards describe processing/review/accuracy, and the assistant remains a visual guide rather than a conversation surface.

The Evaluation workspace extends this language with **operational evidence cues** rather than neutral SaaS cards. Every major card group includes at least one focus-corner, scan-line, measured baseline, verified alignment tick, or document-edge cue. The optical-focus mark appears at a visible scale in primary chrome, with **Alignment Periwinkle #7788FF** reserved for observation points, primary actions, and evaluation focus states. Whenever the assistant appears, it remains a recognizable compact scanning companion and its bubble references the current teacher task rather than generic encouragement.

Results workspace surfaces use **document-edge evidence rails** and measured numeric typography instead of intrusive line ornaments. Score summaries use JetBrains Mono, while teacher-facing headings remain in Inter; student roll numbers are treated as measurable identifiers rather than decorative labels. Teacher flow remains primary—class, student, question, explanation—with any processing evidence explicitly secondary and never required to understand an awarded mark.

## Chosen Approach — Measured Softness

**Design Movement:** Soft instrumental product UI with editorial developer-tool restraint. It pairs the comforting surfaces and small companion character of a premium productivity app with the hierarchy and legibility expected of a research workflow.

**Core Principles:** The interface should keep the primary task visible, use calm whitespace as a functional surface, make technical metrics feel comprehensible rather than intimidating, and use warmth only where it guides attention. Every decorative detail must clarify an interaction, establish place, or reduce perceived complexity.

**Color Philosophy:** Pale lavender-blue sits outside the main workspace as a quiet, low-pressure frame. Warm white panels preserve reading comfort for long comparison sessions. Ink-black typography gives metrics and text diffs a crisp operational feel, while a restrained periwinkle accent establishes interactive focus and muted teal/amber/rose distinguish evaluation states without relying on color alone.

**Layout Paradigm:** A persistent tool rail and compact top header frame an asymmetric canvas. The landing hero uses a left-aligned story with a floating assistant cluster on the right; feature cards deliberately step through the lower edge rather than forming a sterile centered grid. The workspace prioritizes broad editable text panels, then flows into analysis rather than presenting a dense dashboard at first glance.

**Signature Elements:** A pale field of barely visible scanning guides; deliberately offset white cards with softly rounded structural corners; and a compact ceramic scanning companion with a concise speech bubble. A fine scan-line motif appears as a border or rule, not as decorative noise.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Controls are direct, labelled, and compact. Hover and press feedback is short and physical, while comparison feedback uses a brief, quiet analyzing state. Placeholder navigation makes its unavailable status explicit rather than suggesting a broken route.

**Animation:** Use 120–220ms ease-out transitions for control feedback, with a small 30–60ms stagger only for initial card reveal. The assistant and background remain still except for a subtle reduced-motion-safe float. No looping visual spectacle, bounce, or animated gradients.

**Typography System:** DM Sans provides warm, high-clarity interface text with a strong 600–700 weight display hierarchy. JetBrains Mono is reserved for source text, numbers where alignment matters, and comparison diffs. Headlines are compact and left-aligned; supporting copy stays narrow enough to preserve a composed reading rhythm.

**Brand Essence:** A calm, visual OCR evaluation workspace for teams that need to prove how well extracted text matches the source. **Personality:** precise, approachable, composed.

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are clear, operational, and quietly confident; CTAs describe the next action precisely; microcopy anticipates the evaluator’s workflow. Example lines: “Know exactly where recognition slips.” and “Compare two texts. Keep the evidence.” Generic welcome language is excluded.

**Wordmark & Logo:** The mark combines an optical focus corner, a measured text baseline, and a verified alignment tick. It is a compact dark geometry with one periwinkle observation point, appearing at a visible scale in the rail and header.

**Signature Brand Color:** **Alignment Periwinkle — #7788FF.**

### Authentication Surface Decisions

Unauthenticated screens use **Alignment Periwinkle #7788FF** for their principal CTA and interactive focus treatment. Each public-access surface carries an explicit source-evidence motif—focus corners, a measured scan rule, a periwinkle observation point, or an aligned document-status tile—so the product remains recognizably EvalAI before a teacher reaches the workspace. EvalBot is framed as a compact scanning companion with source-sheet alignment and capture-status cues, never as a general chatbot.

Authentication entry points now use a **prominent source-sheet signal tile** with measured baseline rules, an alignment state, and a document-edge corner. Teacher account copy anchors account creation in question-paper evidence, marking context, and review readiness rather than generic onboarding. The larger optical-focus mark repeats the same observation-point and baseline geometry on the form surface.
